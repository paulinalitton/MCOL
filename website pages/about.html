Our Passion & Purpose

At Music City Outdoor Lighting, we're passionate about creating outdoor spaces that make the Nashville spark. With over ten years of experience, our team blends expert design in low-voltage lighting and audio with the lively energy of Music City.

Just like every note in a song comes together to create something beautiful, we pay close attention to every little detail — helping you create your own outdoor paradise that looks incredible and feels like a true reflection of your style and story.

How It Works

Simple. Collaborative.
Perfected.

Our team makes bringing your outdoor vision to life simple and stress-free. We guide you every step of the way — from understanding your initial dreams to expert installation and fine-tuning. We make sure everything is perfect so you can simply relax and enjoy your beautiful new space.

01

Share Your Ideas

Tell us your vision, your lifestyle, and what you love about your property. Every great design starts with a conversation, not a sales pitch.

02

We Visit Your Property

Our designer walks your grounds at dusk to see exactly how light behaves on your architecture, trees, and landscape features.

03

Custom Design & Plan

We create a tailored lighting composition — specific fixture placement, beam angles, and color temperature — unique to your home.

04

Expert Installation

Our licensed crew installs with precision, keeping your property clean and your turf undisturbed throughout the entire process.

05

Ready to Enjoy

We walk through every zone with you at night, make final adjustments, and hand you a system that just works — beautifully, every time.
