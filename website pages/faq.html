System & Technology

What is low-voltage outdoor lighting?

+
Low-voltage outdoor lighting operates at 12V DC, stepped down from standard 120V household current by a transformer. The low voltage makes the system safe to work with, dramatically more energy-efficient than line-voltage alternatives, and straightforward to expand or reconfigure later. All of our residential systems are 12V, and every wire run is hub-fed for clean, serviceable wiring.

How long do the LEDs last?

+
The LED modules we install are rated for 50,000 hours — roughly 25 years at 6 hours per night. In practice, most bulbs last the life of the fixture without replacement. If a lamp ever does fail, it swaps out in minutes. We use lamps with a consistent 2700K–3000K warm white spectrum so the system stays visually coherent as individual bulbs are replaced over time.

What fixtures and materials do you use?

+
We use solid brass and marine-grade copper fixtures from professional-grade lines. Brass naturally patinas over time to a warm antique tone that blends into planting beds. All transformers are heavy-duty multi-zone units with built-in surge protection, astronomic timers, and smart-home integration ports. We do not install plastic fixtures.
Design & Consultation

What happens during the free consultation?

+
We walk your property at dusk — the only time lighting decisions make real sense. We discuss which features matter most to you (curb appeal, safety, entertaining), identify zones worth illuminating, and sketch a fixture layout on-site. You leave with a clear picture of what the finished system will look like and a written quote before we go. No pressure, no obligation.

How do I choose the right package?

+

Most clients land in one of three tiers based on scope:

Essentials — focused lighting on one or two zones (facade + path, or landscape only). Great for first-time buyers or entry-level curb appeal.

Signature — full-property coverage including facade, landscape, paths, and hardscape. Our most popular package for residential homes in the $600K–$2M range.

Estate — comprehensive multi-zone system with entertainment lighting, pool and water features, outdoor audio integration, and smart-home connectivity.

Our estimator tool can suggest a starting point, or we can assess your property directly during consultation.

+

The system also conveys care and permanence to buyers in a way that fresh paint or new mulch cannot.

Installation

How long does installation take?

+
Most full-property systems are installed in a single day — typically 6 to 10 hours depending on property size. Our crew arrives with all materials pre-staged, handles wiring, fixture placement, transformer programming, and testing in one continuous visit. We schedule a dusk walkthrough at the end of installation day to fine-tune beam angles and brightness zones with you present.

Will installation damage my lawn or landscaping?

+
Low-voltage wiring requires minimal ground disturbance. Cable runs are typically buried 4 to 6 inches using a narrow trenching tool or hand-laid under mulch and edging, with no damage to mature roots. Fixture stakes push into softened soil by hand. We restore all disturbed areas before we leave — mulch is re-raked, sod is re-laid, and the lawn should look untouched by the time we're done.

Can I add lighting to an existing system or expand later?

+
Yes, and it is one of the advantages of low-voltage systems. Our hub-based wiring architecture is designed for expansion — additional fixture runs connect back to the transformer without disturbing existing zones. We can add lighting to a pool area, new planting bed, or expanded patio at any time. We also service and expand systems installed by other contractors if the wiring and transformer are compatible.

Can you light a dock, pool area, or water feature?

+
Yes. Pool surrounds, dock decking, waterfall features, and fountains are all zones we design for regularly. Underwater and wet-location fixtures use IP68-rated submersible LEDs. Dock and pool lighting is typically integrated into the same transformer system as the rest of the property, with its own dedicated zone for independent dimming and scheduling.
Smart Controls

Can I control my lights from my phone?

+
Yes. All of our transformer systems include Wi-Fi connectivity and are compatible with iOS and Android control apps. You can turn zones on or off, dim individual areas, create scenes, and override schedules from anywhere. Our systems also integrate with Lutron, Control4, and most major smart-home platforms if you have a whole-home automation system.

Can the lights change color for the holidays?

+
Yes. We offer RGBW (red, green, blue, white) LED fixtures that allow full color control. Holiday presets — warm red and green for Christmas, red and blue for July 4th, orange and purple for Halloween — are selectable in the app with a single tap. RGBW fixtures can be added to any zone and are a popular upgrade on facade and tree lighting. The default is always a beautiful warm white; color is available when you want it.

How do astronomic timers work?

+
Our transformers include GPS-synced astronomic timers that automatically calculate local sunrise and sunset times for your specific location and adjust the on/off schedule every day of the year. The lights come on at dusk and turn off at whatever time you choose — midnight, 2 a.m., or sunrise — without you ever touching a setting. Seasonal schedule drift is eliminated entirely.
Outdoor Audio

Can you add an outdoor audio system alongside the lighting?

+
Yes, and it is one of our most popular add-ons. We install landscape speaker systems alongside or independent of lighting projects. Speakers are positioned in planting beds, under eaves, or in hardscape and connected to a weatherproof amplifier and streaming source. Audio and lighting are often run in a single installation visit, which keeps disruption to a minimum.

Will my neighbors be able to hear my outdoor audio system?

+
Not if the system is designed correctly. Distributed low-volume speakers placed close to the listening area deliver clear, immersive sound at levels that do not carry over property lines. The goal is to fill your space with music — not broadcast to the neighborhood. We design speaker placement and aim with privacy in mind, and we walk you through the coverage zone before leaving.

Can I control the music from my phone?

+
Yes. Our audio systems connect to streaming platforms — Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, and others — via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Volume, source, and zone control are available through the amplifier's native app or through smart-home integrations. If you already use a whole-home audio platform like Sonos or Control4 Audio, we can integrate directly into it.

How are the speakers hidden outdoors?

+
Landscape speakers are designed to disappear. Rock-style enclosures nestle into planting beds and look like natural stone. Surface-mount speakers in olive, brown, or grey blend into siding, soffits, and fencing. In-ground subwoofers are flush with the lawn. All speaker placements are shown and approved during the design phase — nothing goes in the ground without your sign-off on location and appearance.

What audio package is best for outdoor entertaining?

+
For a patio, pool deck, or outdoor kitchen setup, we typically recommend a 4- to 6-speaker stereo zone with a weatherproof amplifier and a subwoofer for low-end presence. This covers a 600–1,200 sq ft entertaining area with full, balanced sound at conversation-friendly volumes. Larger properties or those with multiple outdoor zones benefit from a multi-zone system with independent volume control per area — so the pool zone and the front porch run different sources at different volumes simultaneously.
Service & Warranty

What warranty do you offer?

+
All installations include a 2-year workmanship warranty covering wiring, connections, and fixture placement. Fixtures and transformers carry the manufacturer's warranty — typically 5 years on transformers and lifetime on solid brass and copper housings. LED lamp modules carry a 3-year warranty. If anything fails in the first two years, we come back and fix it at no charge.

What protection plans do you offer?

+
Our Protection Plan covers annual inspection, seasonal angle re-tuning as plantings grow, bulb replacement, transformer testing, and priority scheduling for service calls. The plan is included at no additional cost with all Signature and Estate packages. For Essentials packages and systems installed elsewhere, Protection Plans are available as an annual add-on. Ask your consultant for current plan pricing.

How do I schedule a service visit or tune-up?

+
Call or text us at 615-905-1905 or use the contact form on our website. Protection Plan clients receive priority scheduling, typically within 48 hours. Non-plan service visits are scheduled within 5–7 business days depending on the season. We recommend a tune-up every 12–18 months as plants grow and change the angles light lands on surfaces.
