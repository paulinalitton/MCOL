Lights Installed

Christmas light installation in Nashville is a full-service rental, not a product purchase. We supply commercial-grade C9 and mini-LED strands, design the layout to fit your specific rooflines, gables, columns, and trees, then send a crew with proper safety gear to hang it all — no climbing your ladder, no untangling last year's strands. Lights are clipped (never stapled) to gutters and shingles to protect the roof envelope.

Mid-season, if a strand fails or a bulb dies, you text us and we come out. After the holidays, we return on a scheduled date to take it all down, coil it properly, and store it in our warehouse until next November. Most clients lock in a recurring slot every year and never think about holiday lights again — including commercial properties, HOA entrances, and historic-district homes where the install has to be done right.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How much does professional Christmas light installation cost in Nashville?

    +
    Most residential holiday light installations range from $600 to $4,000+, depending on home size, coverage scope, and yard display features. Our Essentials package starts at $600 for roofline and basic tree wraps. Schedule a free consultation for a personalized quote.

    Do you provide the lights, or do I need to buy my own?

    +
    We provide everything — commercial-grade, professional-quality LED lights that are far superior to retail options. Because we store and reuse the lights year to year, you don't need to purchase or maintain any equipment. Everything is included in your package price.

    When do you install and take down the lights?

    +
    We begin installations in early November and try to have all homes complete before Thanksgiving. Takedown is scheduled in January, typically within the first two weeks after the New Year. We'll schedule your specific dates at the time of booking.

    What if a bulb burns out or something stops working mid-season?

    +
    We include midseason service visits and will come back at no charge if anything stops working during the holiday season. Our commercial-grade lights rarely fail, but if they do, we fix it promptly. Your display stays perfect all season.

    Do you store the lights after takedown?

    +
    Yes. After takedown, we carefully pack and store all lights, clips, and display elements for you. When next season arrives, everything comes out of storage clean and ready to install. No boxes in your attic, no tangled strands, no stress.

    Can I customize the colors and style of my display?

    +
    Absolutely. We offer warm white, cool white, multicolor, and custom color combinations. During your design consultation, we'll go over all options and create a display that matches your vision — from classic traditional to modern minimalist to full festive color.

    What areas of Nashville do you serve for Christmas lights?

    +
    Our Christmas light installation routes cover most of Davidson, Williamson, and Sumner counties — Belle Meade, Green Hills, Forest Hills, Brentwood, Franklin, Bellevue, Hendersonville, Gallatin, Spring Hill, Lebanon, and Mt. Juliet. Holiday slots fill by early October, so book your address early; commercial properties further out are negotiable.

    When should I book to guarantee a slot?

    +
    We recommend booking by October to secure your preferred installation date. November slots fill up very quickly — especially for the week before Thanksgiving. Returning clients get first right of refusal on their previous date each year.
