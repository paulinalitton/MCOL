The city of Forest Hills is one of the more demanding outdoor lighting environments in Middle Tennessee. This separately incorporated city south of Nashville is defined by 1–2 acre estate lots, long curved driveways, and a near-continuous oak-and-hickory canopy that drops the ambient light noticeably even at midday. Designing one balanced system for a property off Tyne Boulevard or Granny White Pike means accounting for façade, specimen trees, drive court, entertaining zones, and a hundred yards of shaded approach — all reading together as a single layered nightscape.

Our Forest Hills builds start with a dusk site walk, never a fixture catalog. We layer ground-up specimen uplights against discreet path runs, integrate any existing tennis court or pool lighting, and run the whole system on properly sized transformers tied to astronomic timers that track Forest Hills' true sunset behind the ridge. Wire pathways are mapped around heritage root zones before any trench opens, and we coordinate with your arborist on any pruning near sight lines. The result holds calibration through summer leaf-out and winter dormancy alike.

Local Considerations for Forest Hills

Forest Hills is unusual in Middle Tennessee for being a separately incorporated city — not a Nashville neighborhood — with its own municipal posture toward dark-sky-aware lighting and minimal glare. We design every full-property system with that in mind: shielded fixtures, fully concealed wire routes, and brightness curves tuned down rather than up. On 1–2 acre lots off Vaughn's Gap Road and Otter Creek, transformers are oversized so the system can be expanded later without re-trenching, and every install factors arborist coordination as a line item, not an afterthought.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do you design a full-property system for a Forest Hills lot?

    +
    We treat the entire 1–2 acre parcel as one composition: long approach drive, façade, specimen oaks, terraces, and any pool or court lighting. Site walks happen at dusk so we can calibrate against the actual canopy density on your property — that varies dramatically between a lot off Tyne Boulevard and one further up Vaughn's Gap. Transformers are sized to allow future expansion without re-trenching.

    Does Forest Hills have specific dark-sky or glare ordinances?

    +
    The city of Forest Hills is separately incorporated from Nashville and the residential lighting culture leans dark-sky-aware: shielded fixtures, downward-aimed beams, no spill onto adjoining properties. Our default Forest Hills designs already meet that posture. We use fully shielded brass fixtures, tune brightness downward, and route every cable underground so daytime sightlines stay completely clean.

    Can you integrate existing tennis court or pool lighting?

    +
    Yes — and on many Forest Hills properties we're doing exactly that. We tie existing pool, sport court, and pavilion lighting into the same astronomic controller as the new landscape and façade work, so the whole property comes on and off as one event rather than five separate timers. Existing line-voltage zones stay on their original circuits; new work runs low-voltage in parallel.
