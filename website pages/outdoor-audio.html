Outdoor audio in Nashville is an acoustics problem, not a speaker-shopping problem. Open yards have no walls to reflect sound, so the coverage map has to be plotted before the gear list — how many speakers, at what heights, aimed in which directions, on how many zones, so the patio, pool deck, and back lawn each get even sound without one zone bleeding into the next. We design that map first, then specify the hardware.

Typical builds use disguised rock or planter speakers along the yard perimeter, in-ground subwoofers buried at hardscape edges for warmth without visible boxes, and weather-resistant amplifiers in the garage or pool equipment closet. Everything is zoned and app-controlled from your phone, integrates with Sonos, Apple AirPlay, and most smart-home systems, and is wired to survive Tennessee summers and ice storms alike.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How much does an outdoor audio system cost in Nashville?

    +
    Most residential outdoor audio systems range from $1,500 to $8,000+, depending on the number of zones, speaker type, and smart home integration. A simple 2-zone patio system starts in the Essentials range; whole-property multi-zone systems with integrated lighting and automation are quoted on a custom basis.

    What types of outdoor speakers do you install?

    +
    We install landscape rock speakers (disguised as decorative rocks), in-ground flush-mount speakers, bollard speakers (short post-mounted), in-ceiling speakers for covered patios and pergolas, and buried subwoofers for low-frequency reinforcement. Speaker type is selected based on your property layout and coverage goals.

    Will outdoor speakers survive Nashville summers and winters?

    +
    Yes. All speakers we install are rated for outdoor environments — designed to handle humidity, UV exposure, rain, and temperature swings. We specify components from brands with proven track records in outdoor applications, and we test every installation before sign-off.

    Can I control the system from my phone or smart home?

    +
    Absolutely. We integrate with Sonos, Apple AirPlay 2, Amazon Echo, Google Home, and more advanced platforms like Control4 and Crestron. You can control individual zones, adjust volume, and stream from any source — all from your phone or voice assistant.

    Can outdoor audio be installed alongside my existing landscape lighting?

    +
    Yes, and it's often the ideal approach. Routing speaker cabling alongside existing lighting wiring reduces excavation, protects your landscaping, and creates a more cohesive system. We regularly add audio to existing lighting installations or design both systems together from scratch.

    What areas of Nashville do you serve for outdoor audio?

    +
    Outdoor audio installs run weekly across Davidson, Williamson, and Sumner counties — Belle Meade, Green Hills, Forest Hills, Brentwood, Franklin, Bellevue, Hendersonville, Gallatin, Spring Hill, Lebanon, and Mt. Juliet. Commercial venues — restaurant patios, pool clubs, event spaces — we'll travel further; just ask.

    How long does outdoor audio installation take?

    +
    Most residential outdoor audio systems are installed in a single day. Our crew arrives with all materials, runs cabling, mounts or buries speakers, connects amplification, and configures your control app before leaving. You're ready to use the system the same evening.

    Do you offer maintenance plans for outdoor audio systems?

    +
    Yes. Annual system checks ensure speaker performance, amplifier health, and connection integrity. We can also update firmware, reconfigure zones, and add speakers to an existing system as your outdoor space evolves. Maintenance plans are available separately or bundled with lighting service plans.
