Security lighting in Nashville fails in two specific ways: it glares so hard neighbors complain, or it leaves the back alley pitch-black where it actually matters. Our designs solve both. Motion-sensor LED floodlights cover driveways and back yards with adjustable detection zones; dusk-to-dawn perimeter lights wash side yards, gates, and the dark wedge between house and fence; concealed step lights remove tripping hazards without screaming "security camera property."

Every system is built to coordinate with your existing cameras — Ring, Nest, Eufy, or a hardwired DVR. Fixtures are mounted at heights and angles that improve nighttime camera image quality (low-color-temperature LEDs in the 3000K range, not the harsh blue 5000K cheap floodlights use). The same lights that deter intruders also make the property feel cared-for from the curb — which itself is a deterrent.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How much does security lighting cost in Nashville?

    +
    Most residential security lighting projects range from $1,200 to $7,000+, depending on property size, number of zones, and smart integration requirements. Our Essentials package starts at $1,200 for entry, pathway, and basic flood coverage. Schedule a free consultation for a personalized quote.

    Do motion floodlights actually deter intruders?

    +
    Studies consistently show that well-lit properties are significantly less likely to be targeted. Motion-activated floodlights are especially effective because they startle and expose anyone approaching after dark. Combined with dusk-to-dawn perimeter lighting, they eliminate the dark zones intruders rely on.

    Can security lighting integrate with my existing cameras?

    +
    Yes. We design lighting zones to complement camera fields of view, ensuring that when motion triggers your cameras, there's sufficient light for clear footage. Our Estate package includes full camera-zone integration planning. We work with most major camera brands.

    Will the lights annoy my neighbors or bother wildlife?

    +
    Thoughtful placement and shielding ensures light stays on your property and doesn't spill into neighboring lots or skyward. Motion sensors are calibrated to avoid triggering on wildlife where possible. We also offer warm-spectrum LEDs that are less disruptive to nocturnal animals.

    What areas of Nashville do you serve?

    +
    Security lighting installs run weekly across Davidson, Williamson, and Sumner counties — Belle Meade, Green Hills, Brentwood, Franklin, Forest Hills, Bellevue, Hendersonville, Gallatin, Spring Hill, Lebanon, and Mt. Juliet. We also handle commercial perimeter lighting beyond that radius — ask about your specific address.

    Can I control the lights from my phone?

    +
    Yes. Our systems are compatible with most major smart home platforms including Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Apple HomeKit, and Control4. You can schedule zones, trigger lights remotely, and receive notifications when motion sensors activate.

    How long does installation take?

    +
    Most residential security lighting systems are installed in a single day (6–8 hours). We arrive with all materials and complete fixture placement, wiring, testing, and sensor programming. A night-one calibration walkthrough is included to fine-tune all motion zones with you present.

    Do you offer maintenance plans?

    +
    Yes. Annual maintenance plans include fixture inspection, bulb and sensor replacement, motion calibration, and system testing. Keeping your security lighting well-maintained ensures it performs reliably when it matters most.
